    How Chat Club works

    Here's what everything does!

    🎨
    Customize
    Change your name, pick your own color, and choose a background look for the whole app.
    🚪
    Log out
    Signs you out. You'll need your name and password to log back in.
    ➕
    Start a new group chat
    Makes a brand new chat and lets you give it a name.
    📨
    Invites
    Shows up when someone adds you to a chat. Tap ✓ Join to hop in, or ✕ No thanks to skip it.
    💬
    A chat in your list
    Tap it to open that conversation.
    ✏️
    Rename
    Change that chat's name — everyone in it will see the new name.
    ➕
    Add friend
    Type a friend's name to invite them to this chat.
    🗑️
    Delete
    Only shows up if you created the chat. Deletes it for everyone — can't be undone!
    ➤
    Message box & send
    Type a message, then tap the arrow (or press Enter) to send it.
    👆
    Hold down a message you sent
    Lets you edit it.
    🙂+
    React
    Add an emoji reaction to any message.
    ↩️
    Reply
    Reply directly to a specific message.

    Make it feel like yours!
